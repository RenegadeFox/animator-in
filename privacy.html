Article 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, “the Site” means Animator In.com, a pretend streaming site, including every page, button, poster, loading screen and stick figure appearing on it, whether now existing or hereafter drawn.

1.2 “You” means any person, kid, grown-up, pet, robot or sentient stick figure who looks at the Site, or who has been looked at by the Site, or who is thinking about looking at the Site.

1.3 “We”, “Us” and “Our” mean Finn, together with his successors, assigns, and any friends he lets help.

1.4 “Teal” means the light blue stick figure with glasses, who lives inside the Ask Teal button and who is thinking very hard at all times.

1.5 “The Cookie Man” means a cookie shaped like a man, as more fully described in Article 15. He is not real.

1.6 “Browser Memory” means the little space inside Your own browser where the Site writes things down for a while. Nobody else can read it.

1.7 Headings are for fun only and shall not affect the meaning of this Agreement, which also has no meaning. See the Disclaimer.

Article 2. Acceptance of Terms

2.1 By looking at the Site, clicking on the Site, scrolling the Site, or standing near a screen on which the Site is shown, You hereby accept every term of this Agreement, including the terms You have not read, which is all of them.

2.2 If You do not accept this Agreement, You may press the Back button. The Back button is Your friend. It is located in the top left corner and has an arrow.

2.3 Acceptance is deemed to occur at the exact moment You say the words “wait, what?” while reading this document.

Article 3. Information We Collect (Not Much)

3.1 While the Site is open, Your Browser Memory may hold: (a) the name You picked; (b) Your profile picture; (c) Your background colour or gradient; (d) a list of which episodes You watched at least halfway; and (e) the place where You stopped watching, for the purposes of “Continue watching”.

3.2 All information described in Section 3.1 stays inside Your own browser. It is never sent to Us, to GitHub, to Alan Becker, to the moon, or to any other place or person.

3.3 The password and email You type on the login page are checked by the Site and then immediately forgotten, like a dream or a sandwich You had last Tuesday.

3.4 We do not knowingly collect information about Your favourite colour, except when You pick it on purpose for Your background, which is allowed.

Article 4. How We Use Information

4.1 Your name is used to say Your name. Your picture is used to show Your picture. We are very proud of this system.

4.2 The list of watched episodes is used by Teal, and only by Teal, to decide which show to recommend. Teal agrees to use this power only for good, and for snacks.

4.3 The place where You stopped watching is used for one thing: so that You can carry on from there. It is not used for Spying, Sneaking, Snooping or Sock Collecting.

5.1 The Site does not use cookies of any kind. The edible kind are discussed separately in Article 15.

5.2 For the avoidance of doubt, no cookies, crumbs, biscuits, or cracker-adjacent products shall be placed on Your device. Please do not put real cookies in Your iPad.

Article 6. Sharing and Selling

6.1 We do not sell Your information. We do not rent it. We do not lend it. We do not trade it for Pokémon cards, even rare ones.

6.2 We do not sell Your name, Your shows, or even a single stick figure, whether red, green, yellow, blue, orange or purple.

6.3 If any person offers Us a very large pile of money for Your Browser Memory, We shall say “no thank you” in a polite voice, and then We shall say it again in a funny voice.
