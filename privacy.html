Animator In.com Privacy Policy

(the pretend version)

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. What is this place?

Animator In.com is a pretend streaming site made by Finn, just for fun. It is a fan site. It is not the real Alan Becker, and it is not made by or connected to Alan Becker or his studio. The shows belong to Alan Becker. Finn added his own voices to the episodes, and the original credits stay in the videos.

2. What we remember about you

While the site is open, your browser remembers:

  • The name you picked
  • Your profile picture and background
  • Which episodes you watched at least halfway
  • Where you stopped, for “Continue watching”

It stays inside your own browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it goes away when you close the site or log in again.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell anything. Not your name, not your shows, not even a single stick figure.
  • We do not save the password or email you type on the login page. The page only checks them and forgets them.
  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not have a secret team of stick figures watching you through the screen.

4. Teal

Teal picks shows by looking at the list of what you watched, right inside your browser. Teal does not tell anyone what you watched. Teal is just thinking really hard.

5. The boring-but-true part

This site is kept on GitHub. Like every website, the computers that host it can see normal visit information, such as that a page was loaded. We do not look at it.

6. Rules for everyone

By using Animator In.com, you agree that animation is awesome and that you will not take the credit for someone else's video.

7. The Cookie Man

We promise we will not throw milk at you if you buy a cookie man.
